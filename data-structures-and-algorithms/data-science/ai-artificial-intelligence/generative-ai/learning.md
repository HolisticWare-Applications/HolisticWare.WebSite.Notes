    Generative AI (Gen AI) agents are autonomous software systems powered by foundation models that can perceive inputs, reason through multi-step plans, and use external tools or APIs to complete complex tasks on behalf of users. Unlike standard generative AI that simply responds passively to text prompts, agents actively execute workflows and make decisions.Core ComponentsThe Brain (Foundation Model): Large language models like Gemini process human intent, direct the logic, and formulate plans.Reasoning Loop: The continuous cycle of observing data, planning next steps, acting, and evaluating results (often using frameworks like ReAct).Tools and APIs: Software functions that let the agent read databases, run code, search the web, or send messages.Memory: Short-term and long-term storage mechanisms (such as vector databases) that track conversation history and past execution states.Key CapabilitiesAutonomy: Operates independently over long stretches without needing constant human prompts at every single step.Self-Correction: Detects errors during execution and alters its path or retries actions until the goal is met.Multi-Agent Collaboration: Divides massive tasks among specialized sub-agents (e.g., a planner agent passing work to a writer agent).If you're looking to build or use one, let me know:Are you interested in frameworks (like LangChain or LangGraph)?Do you want a specific use case (like customer support or data analysis)?What programming language do you prefer?11 sitesGen AI Agents: Transform Your Organization - Google Skills... circle Gen AI for customer engagement · circle Use case: Customer Experience Agent Studio · circle Build your own agent · circ...Google SkillsWhat are AI agents?
