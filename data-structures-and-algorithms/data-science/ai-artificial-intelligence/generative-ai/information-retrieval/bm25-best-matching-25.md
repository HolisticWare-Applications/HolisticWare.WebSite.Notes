# BM25 (Best Matching 25) 

bm25-best-matching-25.md

*   industry-standard ranking algorithm used by search engines (like Elasticsearch) 

    *   to estimate a document's relevance to a query. 
    
    *   upgrades traditional keyword matching by factoring in 
    
        *   term frequency (saturation)
        
        *   inverse document frequency (rarity)
        
        *   document length normalization.
